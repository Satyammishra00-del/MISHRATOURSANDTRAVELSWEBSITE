Our Legacy & Promise

Your Journey, Our Responsibility

For over a decade, Mishra Tours And Travels has been the trusted travel and mobility partner for thousands of families, pilgrimage groups, and corporate enterprises across Maharashtra and India.

Founded in Boisar, Serving Pan-India

Built on Faith, Service, and Uncompromising Safety

What began in Boisar, Palghar as a dedicated regional passenger transport initiative has blossomed into one of Maharashtra's most respected full-service travel companies. Founded by Shivam Mishra and Satyam Mishra, our brand was built on a simple yet unshakeable ethos: every guest travels like family.

Whether it is organizing complex multi-day sacred Yatras to Kedarnath and Badrinath, curating relaxing family holidays in Goa and Kerala, or providing immaculate 26-seater luxury Tempo Travellers for corporate events, we guarantee prompt dispatch, verified chauffeurs, transparent pricing, and 24/7 personal availability.

Our Milestones

A Decade of Continuous Excellence

From our first bus in Boisar to a renowned pan-India tour network.

2014 - Inception

Founded in Boisar, Maharashtra

Shivam Mishra and Satyam Mishra initiated travel operations with private charter cabs and mini buses serving local industrial staff, wedding groups, and pilgrimage travelers from Boisar and Palghar.

2017 - Fleet Expansion

Induction of Luxury Tempo Travellers

Acquired premium Force 17-seater & 26-seater Tempo Travellers with luxury pushback seating to cater to high-demand leisure trips to Goa, Mahabaleshwar, Shirdi, and Rajasthan.

2020 - Sacred Char Dham & Kedarnath Specialization

Pan-India Spiritual Tour Department

Established dedicated pilgrimage handling teams in Haridwar, Rishikesh, and Guptkashi. Coordinated helicopter bookings, senior-citizen assistance, and Sattvic boarding for over 3,000 devotees.

2023 - Corporate Travel & Luxury Coaches

55-Seater Volvo & BharatBenz Coaches

Partnered with manufacturing plants in Tarapur MIDC and corporate houses in Mumbai for routine plant transfers, national offsites, and destination wedding transport.

2026 - Present & Beyond

Digital Booking & VIP Force Urbania Fleet

Launched full online booking suite with direct WhatsApp integration, 10 flagship Indian circuits, and next-generation Force Urbania luxury vans.
