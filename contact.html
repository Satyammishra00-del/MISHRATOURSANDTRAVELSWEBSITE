Frequently Asked Questions

How far in advance should we book vehicles?

For weekend getaways or local outstation trips, 24 to 48 hours notice is sufficient. For peak holiday seasons (Diwali, New Year, May) and Kedarnath Yatras, we recommend booking 2 to 4 weeks in advance.

Are driver allowances and tolls included?

In all our all-inclusive tour packages, driver allowance, tolls, and parking are 100% included. For raw vehicle kilometer rentals, driver allowance is charged transparently per day as stated in the quote.

Can senior citizens join the Kedarnath Yatra?

Yes! We specialize in senior citizen pilgrimage care. We assist in advance biometric registration, arrange helicopter shuttles from Phata/Guptkashi, or arrange pony/doli services, with round-the-clock group coordinator support.
